Privacy Policy

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers, with one optional exception you control: the Network Overview "Public IP" lookup contacts ipinfo.io only when you run it — see Third-Party Services below.

Traffic Monitor and Wi-Fi Capture. These require explicit consent before first use, and the two consents are SEPARATE because the two tools see different things: Traffic Monitor sees this computer's own traffic, while Wi-Fi Capture sees management frames broadcast by every device in range — including probe requests, which can carry the names of networks a device has joined before. Capture runs only while you have started it and stops when you stop it. The App does not include Wireshark and does not install it; it detects a copy you installed yourself and runs it, and it works normally without one.

Investigating display defects. Some display problems only appear on hardware the developer does not have. A known example is a small movement of the window that has been reported on 14-inch laptops and cannot be reproduced on the 15-inch machine the App is developed on — measurement inside the App found no movement at all, so the only remaining way to investigate is to compare the two machines. For that reason, while diagnostic logging is switched on, the App writes a short description of the machine into the log: the Windows version, each display's resolution and scaling, the panel size the display reports about itself, the graphics adapter and its driver version, and whether the side menu is collapsed. It is written when the App starts, when you switch logging on, and when you collapse or expand the side menu.

This information describes the computer, not you: no user name, no account, no serial number, no network data. If diagnostic logging is off — which is how the App ships — none of it is collected at all, and the App does not even ask Windows for it. Like every other log record it stays on your device, and it reaches us only if you export the log yourself from the Logs menu and choose to send it to support@qandix.com. Sending it is entirely your decision, and you can read the exported file before you do: exporting as plain TXT shows you exactly what it contains.

Network Tools. The Network Tools require explicit one-time consent before first use. Every operation is initiated manually by you and is directed only at the hosts/addresses/networks you specify; the App makes no automated or background network requests. Results and any read of the local ARP neighbor cache are shown on-device and are neither stored nor transmitted off-device. Each tool keeps a history of its runs — what you typed and the result exactly as it was shown (up to 1,000 entries per tool) — in the App's data folder on your device, so you can bring a result back later. A result can contain what the tool itself showed you, such as host names, addresses of devices on your network, or your public IP address. Nothing in it is sent anywhere; you can delete entries from each tool's history list or clear the whole history in the Cache Manager. Wi-Fi Capture keeps no history.

2. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate analytics SDKs, advertising networks, social media SDKs, or cloud services. All third-party code is open-source libraries (Apache POI, zip4j, JNA, Compose Multiplatform) running entirely on-device.

Public IP lookup (optional). When you run "Look up Public IP" in Network Overview, the App makes a single HTTPS request to ipinfo.io (fallback api.ipify.org) to show your public IP address, ISP and approximate location. Those services necessarily see your IP address, governed by their own privacy policies (ipinfo.io/privacy-policy). This is the only feature that contacts an external service, it never runs on its own, and no other personal data is sent.

Microsoft Store billing. Optional paid features (the Survey Pro monthly subscription and Store add-ons) are purchased through the Microsoft Store, governed by Microsoft's Privacy Statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com). We do not receive, store, or process any payment card information; all billing is handled exclusively by Microsoft. The App verifies your purchase status through the Microsoft Store services on your device.

4. Data Storage and Security

• All app data is stored under the App's data folder %APPDATA%\AP-Scout Pro\

• No cloud sync, no remote backup

• Exports (CSV / Excel / ZIP, including the bulk Survey export that mirrors your Project ▸ Site ▸ Building ▸ Floor tree) are written on your device and leave it only when you share them yourself. Excel and ZIP exports can be password-protected (AES-256); passwords are never saved.

• The AP Database is encrypted on-device using AES-256-GCM with PBKDF2 key derivation (600,000 iterations); the key is derived from your passphrase and held only in memory.

• Diagnostic activity logs are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a device-internal key protected by Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI) under your Windows user account; they contain app events only (no personal data), never leave the device unless you export them, and can be exported as plain TXT or a password-protected AES-256 ZIP. The description of your display and graphics hardware, when logging is on, is stored inside that same encrypted log and is protected the same way.

• Licence status obtained from the Microsoft Store is cached in the App's data folder, protected by Windows DPAPI under your Windows user account, so paid features remain available when you are offline. It records which paid options your account holds — never payment details, and never an account identifier — and is not transmitted anywhere.

• Uninstalling the App removes the installed program. For Microsoft Store (MSIX) installs, Windows keeps the App's data inside the app's package profile and removes it as part of uninstall; any remaining data can also be deleted manually under %APPDATA%\AP-Scout Pro\.

5. Subscription and Licence Data

The Survey paid features (floor-plan background, on-map point placement, AP Check Points, point aliases, and up to 5,000 points) require the optional Survey Pro monthly subscription through the Microsoft Store, or a Survey Pro licence supplied through the Microsoft Store by your organization or institution. We do not collect or store payment card information, billing address, or purchase history beyond what the Microsoft Store provides to verify your status on your device. That status is cached on your device (see Data Storage and Security) so paid features keep working offline; it is never sent anywhere.
