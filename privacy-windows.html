Privacy Policy

AP-Scout Pro for Windows ("the App") is developed by Qandix. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Information We Collect

The App accesses the following data solely on your device:

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers, with one optional exception you control: the Network Overview "Public IP" lookup contacts ipinfo.io only when you run it — see Third-Party Services below.

Network Tools. The Network Tools require explicit one-time consent before first use. Every operation is initiated manually by you and is directed only at the hosts/addresses/networks you specify; the App makes no automated or background network requests. Results and any read of the local ARP neighbor cache are shown on-device and are neither stored nor transmitted off-device.

2. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate analytics SDKs, advertising networks, social media SDKs, or cloud services. All third-party code is open-source libraries (Apache POI, zip4j, JNA, Compose Multiplatform) running entirely on-device.

Public IP lookup (optional). When you run "Look up Public IP" in Network Overview, the App makes a single HTTPS request to ipinfo.io (fallback api.ipify.org) to show your public IP address, ISP and approximate location. Those services necessarily see your IP address, governed by their own privacy policies (ipinfo.io/privacy-policy). This is the only feature that contacts an external service, it never runs on its own, and no other personal data is sent.

Microsoft Store billing. Optional paid features (the Survey Pro monthly subscription and Store add-ons) are purchased through the Microsoft Store, governed by Microsoft's Privacy Statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com). We do not receive, store, or process any payment card information; all billing is handled exclusively by Microsoft. The App verifies your purchase status through the Microsoft Store services on your device.

4. Data Storage and Security

• All app data is stored under the App's data folder %APPDATA%\AP-Scout Pro\

• No cloud sync, no remote backup

• Exports (CSV / Excel / ZIP, including the bulk Survey export that mirrors your Project ▸ Site ▸ Building ▸ Floor tree) are written on your device and leave it only when you share them yourself. Excel and ZIP exports can be password-protected (AES-256); passwords are never saved.

• The AP Database is encrypted on-device using AES-256-GCM with PBKDF2 key derivation (600,000 iterations); the key is derived from your passphrase and held only in memory.

• Diagnostic activity logs are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a device-internal key protected by Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI) under your Windows user account; they contain app events only (no personal data), never leave the device unless you export them, and can be exported as plain TXT or a password-protected AES-256 ZIP.

• Uninstalling the App removes the installed program. For Microsoft Store (MSIX) installs, Windows keeps the App's data inside the app's package profile and removes it as part of uninstall; any remaining data can also be deleted manually under %APPDATA%\AP-Scout Pro\.

5. Subscription Data

The Survey paid features (floor-plan background, on-map point placement, AP Check Points, point aliases, and up to 5,000 points) require the optional Survey Pro monthly subscription through the Microsoft Store. We do not collect or store payment card information, billing address, or purchase history beyond what the Microsoft Store provides to verify purchase status on your device.

6. Children's Privacy

This App is designed for professional network engineers and is not intended for use by children under 13.
