Survey vs Survey Pro

Every survey records the same measurements. What a Survey Pro subscription adds is the floor plan underneath them — where each point sits, what it is called, and the ability to keep working on a survey after it is saved.

Survey
20

Included with the app. Full scan data at every point, exported to CSV and Excel.

Survey Pro
5,000

Monthly subscription. Adds the floor plan, named points, AP Check Points, continuous capture and editing after a survey is saved.

If a subscription ends

Nothing is deleted and nothing is hidden. A survey remembers whether it was created with Survey Pro, and that is what decides where you can pick it up again.

  • ✓Open and read every survey — floor plan included.
  • ✓Export anything you captured — CSV, Excel and the portable file.
  • ✓Continue a free survey — within the 20-point limit.
  • —Continue a Pro survey — until the subscription is active again.
  • —Edit a saved survey — the editing tools are unavailable.
  • —Start a new Pro survey — new surveys use the free tier.

Getting Survey Pro

Survey Pro is a monthly subscription managed through your Microsoft account. Current pricing is always the price shown on the Microsoft Store listing, in your own currency. Everything else in AP-Scout Pro is part of the one-time app purchase — no further subscription is required.
